# Skill Go-To-Market

**Get your AI skill listed, priced, and sold on the right marketplaces — without guessing what the platform wants.**

This skill turns any agent (Claude Code, Cursor, OpenClaw, Codex…) into a go-to-market operator for your AI skill products: marketplace selection, listing content, pricing strategy, and launch checklist — built from real hands-on listing experience across Waffo, Agensi, PromptBase, MCP Market and more.

## What it does

| Area | What it covers |
|---|---|
| Marketplace selection | Which platform fits your skill type (agent-skill vs prompt vs tool) |
| Listing content | Title, description, keywords that pass review & convert |
| Pricing strategy | One-time vs subscription, anchoring, tier design |
| Payout & compliance | Region constraints, KYB/KYC, payment rails |
| Launch checklist | What to prepare before you hit publish |

## Why it exists

"Just upload your skill and it sells" is a lie. Every marketplace has its own rules (PromptBase rejects thin prompts, Waffo requires KYB, MCP Market is GitHub-linked), and a listing that ignores those rules gets rejected — or worse, priced wrong and never sells.

This skill encodes the lessons from **actually listing 5+ skills across multiple platforms** (including real rejections and fixes) so you don't burn your first 10 launches learning what should have been obvious.
